## Immediate risk controls

- Do not run `SEED_DEMO_DATA=true` against any database that might contain non-demo records.
- Do not run or repair production migrations until actual migration state, backup/restore, and owner-approved plan are established.
- Do not assume database-backed queue rows guarantee work will execute on serverless infrastructure.
- Do not declare campaign success until the configured provider confirms acceptance and delivery state is tracked.
- Do not provide security or availability sign-off based solely on a successful local build.

The owner confirmed Vercel, Supabase PostgreSQL, and Supabase Storage on 2026-11-03 and approved Phase 1 implementation. After the owner confirmed the Prisma migrations had never been applied outside development, the clean-install sequence was repaired locally and verified against a disposable PostgreSQL 17 database. The linked development database was subsequently migrated with explicit approval; Prisma reports the schema current with no drift, and no seed data was added. No staging/production configuration or services were changed or tested. The separate `AUDIT_REPORT.md` is an earlier code/dependency snapshot: its public subscriber endpoint concern was addressed by consent confirmation and rate limiting before this phase. The latest production dependency scan and remaining advisories are documented in R-18 and [SECURITY_ASSESSMENT.md](./SECURITY_ASSESSMENT.md). Production migration state, worker lifecycle, email delivery, and backup/restore status remain unconfirmed.

## Authorized follow-up status

R-08 was mitigated for new intake in a separately authorized follow-up: public subscriber creation now requires email confirmation and campaign delivery accepts only verified consent. Existing legacy `granted` records are intentionally excluded until the owner approves a reconfirmation/data-reconciliation plan. Other risks remain open.
